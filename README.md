# AI Policy Atlas

Generative-AI policies at US universities and in their computer science courses, synthesized into one policy set.

- **Site:** https://rudrajit1729.github.io/ai-policy-atlas/ (the same site runs locally: double-click `Open AI Policy Atlas.command`, or `python3 -m http.server 8792 --directory v2/site`).
- **Report:** the site's Report tab, or `v2/exports/AI_Policy_Atlas_report.docx`.
- **Data:** every site page links its CSV (`v2/site/data/csv/`); full workbooks in `v2/exports/` (`atlas_v2.xlsx`, `course_layer.xlsx`).

## In numbers
- 774 policy documents from 342 universities; 24,536 statements coded, 23,948 about AI.
- 471 AI passages from CS syllabi at 135 universities; 2,238 statements coded.
- Policy set: 675 institutional policies (207 rules, 404 recommendations, 64 facts and warnings) and 229 CS course policies (165 rules, 35 recommendations, 29 facts and warnings).

## Where things are
- `v2/STATUS_AND_FINDINGS.md`: status and findings
- `v2/DEFINITIONS.md`: definitions of every term
- `v2/METHODS_DECISIONS.md`: decision log (D01 onward)
- `v2/tools/`: pipeline scripts
- `PROVENANCE.md`: what was done, when and how

Original wording in the data is quoted from public university documents, each with a link to its source.
