AI policies for students

US universities and their computer science courses. AI Policy Atlas, November 18, 2026. Website: https://rudrajit1729.github.io/ai-policy-atlas/#students

About this document

This document lists every policy that US university documents and CS course syllabi address to students, drawn from 774 university-wide, school and department documents at 342 universities and from AI passages in CS course syllabi.

A policy is one thing asked of one group. Statements that ask students to do the same thing are merged into one policy, whatever their wording, and each policy is written as a directive to the reader. Within each level, policies are grouped by stance (what the policy does about AI use), then follow the course from before it starts to after it ends, then are grouped by what they ask. The groups come from a thematic analysis by three independent coders. Policies that make the same point appear as one entry led by the most widespread policy, with the others listed as variants and how they differ.

Each entry gives its kind (rule, recommendation, or fact or warning), the number of universities and of statements behind it (“13 (15)” means 15 statements from 13 different universities), the levels where it appears (U university-wide, S school or college, C computing department), and its ID. Sources: every ID links to the website, which lists each statement behind the policy in the institution’s own words, with a link to the source.

Policies addressed to everyone in a university also apply to students; the website lists them on its Other roles page.
